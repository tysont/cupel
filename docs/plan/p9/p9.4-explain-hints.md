# P9.4 — `explain` hints

Branch `p9-explain-hints`. Read `common.md` first, then the plan's `#### P9.4` heading and design §6.5 (`explain`). ADR number: **133**.

## Scope (track (a) item 4)

`explain <finding-id>` today returns the finding with its source excerpt, covering tests, nearest test file, and a test skeleton (`crates/cupel-cli/src/explain.rs`, `finding.action.hints`). Three finding kinds get a remediation hint computed from the facts the finding was built on:

- `clone.cluster` → the common-subtree extraction candidate: the largest normalized subtree every member of the cluster shares (from the `NormTree` fingerprints `static.clones` already computes, ADR-050), rendered as the span in one member plus the list of the other members' matching spans, with the parameters that differ between members named.
- `complexity.high` → the deepest nested block of the function: its span, its nesting depth, and the construct kinds on the path to it, as the extraction candidate that lowers cognitive and cyclomatic complexity most.
- `dep.cycle` → the cheapest edge to cut: for the cycle's edges, the one whose removal breaks the cycle and that carries the fewest imported symbols (from `ModuleGraph` edges and the `Symbols`/imports facts), with the symbols it carries listed so the reader knows what to move.

Each hint is a typed field on the finding's `action.hints` (the envelope and schema change additively), rendered by `explain --format text` and `--format json`, documented by `describe`, and present in the goldens of the fixtures that produce those findings. A hint that cannot be computed (facts missing) is absent, never a placeholder. Record the hint shapes and the choice rules in ADR-133.
